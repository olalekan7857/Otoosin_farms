Before you order

Meat, weight and delivery, clear from the start

Every O'Toosin Farms listing shows the meat type, its condition and the price basis. Nothing is added to your cart until you choose the quantity.

Priced by kilogram

The price shown is for one kilogram. Adjust the quantity to the exact weight you need and the total updates before you add it to your cart.

Condition stated

Fresh, frozen and fried items are labelled on every listing, so you know what you are ordering before it reaches your kitchen.

Delivery paid separately

Product prices exclude delivery. After you order, O'Toosin staff arrange the drop-off and the rider's fee is paid separately.

Two ways to pay

You can choose Pay Now or Pay on Delivery at checkout. Your order is confirmed before anything is prepared.
