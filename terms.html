3. Product information

Product pages show the meat, its available condition, the price per kilogram and other details intended to help you choose. Please review the product name, condition, price and quantity before preparing an order.

Photographs are illustrative. The cut, trimming, packaging and exact appearance of a delivered item can vary from the photograph. Product availability can change, and an item displayed on the website is not a guarantee that it remains available until the order is confirmed.

Nothing on this website is a claim of organic certification, farming-method certification or a guarantee of a particular result from a product.

4. Meat types and states

The shop covers six meat types: pork, chicken, turkey, beef, goat meat and fish. For clarity, pork, beef and goat meat are red meat; chicken and turkey are poultry; fish is seafood.

Where a product-state filter applies, the available states are Fresh, Frozen and Fried. These describe the condition of a product, not a separate meat type. Available conditions can differ by product.

5. Pricing and quantities

Product prices are displayed in Nigerian Naira and are charged per kilogram. When you enter a quantity, it represents the number of kilograms you want. The cart and checkout calculate the product subtotal from the selected price and quantity.

The product subtotal shown at checkout covers the meat products only. It does not include the delivery fee. Prices and availability may change before an order is confirmed; the amount shown for your selection before you complete the ordering process is the amount that applies to that order.

No minimum purchase quantity, fixed weight step or additional product fee applies unless it is expressly shown on the website.

6. Orders and acceptance

Guest checkout is available; you do not need to create an account. The checkout lets you enter your contact and delivery details, choose a payment method and review your selection before completing the order.

Completing the checkout review does not, by itself, mean that the order has been accepted or that a payment has been completed. An order becomes confirmed only when O'Toosin Farms accepts it and communicates that confirmation. If an item is unavailable or a detail needs clarification, the business may contact you before preparing the order.

Please provide accurate information so the order can be reviewed and arranged without avoidable delay.

7. Payment options

The website offers two payment choices for the product amount:

  • Pay Now: pay the product amount online through the payment step provided for the order.
  • Pay on Delivery: pay the product amount to the rider when the order arrives.

O'Toosin Farms does not ask you to send card details by email or chat, and does not itself store full payment-card details. Online payments are handled by the payment provider used for that payment. A payment is not treated as complete until the provider confirms it.

8. Delivery and fees

Delivery is arranged by O'Toosin Farms staff after an order is placed. Delivery details are used to identify where the order should go and to contact you about its delivery. Please provide a complete address, the relevant city or town and state, and any landmark or instruction that will help the rider.

The delivery fee is separate from the product subtotal. It is not added to the product amount shown by the website, and it is paid separately to the rider. The delivery fee depends on the delivery arrangement and will be communicated for the order.

Delivery coverage, timing and any same-day arrangement are not guaranteed by these terms. They are confirmed as part of the order arrangement. If a delivery cannot be completed because the information provided is incomplete or nobody is available to receive the order, O'Toosin Farms or the rider will try to contact you to resolve it.

9. Cancellations, returns, refunds and complaints

If you need to change or cancel an order, contact O'Toosin Farms as soon as possible using a verified contact route where one is available. Whether a change or cancellation is possible depends on whether the order has been confirmed, prepared or handed to a rider.

Meat is a perishable product, so please inspect your order when it arrives and raise any problem promptly. If something is incorrect, damaged or not what you ordered, contact O'Toosin Farms with the order details so the matter can be reviewed.

These terms do not set a fixed cancellation window, return period or refund timeline, and they do not create an automatic refund entitlement. Requests are considered individually in light of the circumstances and applicable law. Nothing in these terms is intended to exclude or limit rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.
