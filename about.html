About us

About O'Toosin Farms

O'Toosin Farms is a meat-focused online shop. We bring pork, chicken, turkey, beef, goat meat and fish together in one storefront so customers can browse the range, choose a product and order by the kilogram.

Our business

One place for the meat you cook with

O'Toosin Farms exists to make buying meat online simpler. Instead of a long list of unrelated products, the shop is organised around the meats customers cook with: pork, chicken, turkey, beef, goat meat and fish.

Each listing brings the meat, its condition and its price per kilogram together. Product state availability varies by item, so every listing shows whether Fresh, Fried or Frozen applies to that cut.

Orders are placed through the website without an account. Customers build a cart, check out as a guest and choose Pay Now or Pay on Delivery for the product total. Our team arranges delivery after checkout, and the delivery fee is settled separately with the rider.
